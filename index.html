1. How do I book a mandi slot for selling my crops?

Navigate to the 'Book Slot' or 'Sell Crop' section from dashboard actions, select your preferred mandi, enter crop details, and generate your token.

2. How are mandi market prices updated?

Market prices are synced directly in real-time with regional government agricultural boards.

3. Is my Kisan Credit Card (KCC) limit viewable online?

Yes, you can view your active KCC credit limits under the 'View All Services' records section.
